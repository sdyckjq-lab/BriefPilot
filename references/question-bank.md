# Question Bank

Ask the fewest questions that improve the brief.

Prefer multiple-choice options when the user may not know how to answer.

Phase 1 verified coverage uses only the universal questions and the landing page / SaaS website questions below. The other question sets are Phase 2 references and are not part of Phase 1 completion.

## Universal Questions

1. Who is the primary audience?
2. What should the viewer do after seeing the design?
3. What must the design communicate in the first 10 seconds?
4. What content, screenshots, claims, or proof points must appear?
5. What visual directions should be avoided?
6. What existing brand assets, `DESIGN.md`, or references should be used?
7. What platform, format, or technical constraints matter?

## Landing Page / SaaS Website

1. Is the page for awareness, signup, demo booking, waitlist, or sales enablement?
2. Is the buyer technical, business, founder, enterprise, consumer, or mixed?
3. Should the page feel like a mature company, founder-led launch, or technical product demo?
4. Which sections are required: hero, pain, solution, workflow, proof, pricing, security, integrations, FAQ, CTA?
5. Are there real product screenshots, customer quotes, metrics, logos, or case studies?

## Deferred Phase 2 Question Sets

The sections below are preserved for Phase 2 expansion. Do not treat them as verified Phase 1 task coverage.

### App Prototype

1. What is the primary user journey?
2. Which screens are required?
3. What states must be shown: empty, loading, error, success, onboarding, upgrade?
4. Should the prototype prioritize product clarity, visual polish, or interaction fidelity?
5. What device or viewport should be targeted?

### Dashboard / Web App

1. What job does the user need to complete repeatedly?
2. What data must be visible by default?
3. What actions must be one click away?
4. What roles or permission levels matter?
5. What edge states must be designed?

### Pitch Deck

1. Who is the audience: investors, customers, partners, internal team?
2. What decision should the deck drive?
3. What is the strongest proof point?
4. What narrative arc should the deck follow?
5. What tone fits: bold, analytical, founder-led, market-research, product-led?

### Social Visual / Infographic

1. Where will it be posted?
2. What is the single takeaway?
3. Should it optimize for authority, virality, clarity, or brand consistency?
4. What format and dimensions are required?
5. What examples should it avoid resembling?
