# Review Checklist

## Page Identity

- [ ] The first screen makes clear this is an AI search research workspace.
- [ ] The visible query, answer confidence, and source origin are understandable without scrolling on desktop.

## Answer And Source Hierarchy

- [ ] The answer body is readable and not visually overwhelmed by metadata.
- [ ] Confidence, source count, and answer state are visible near the answer.
- [ ] Citation markers connect to matching source cards.

## Source Detail

- [ ] Source cards show title, excerpt, system or owner, date, relevance, and link status.
- [ ] Source detail is discoverable on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- [ ] Source-unavailable and permission-blocked states have clear recovery paths.

## Interaction States

- [ ] Search input includes empty, input, submitting, failed, and retry states.
- [ ] Answer area includes empty, generating, success, partial, low-confidence, and failed states.
- [ ] Source list includes empty, loading, available, and source-unavailable states.
- [ ] Save/share controls include idle, working, success, and failed states.

## Visual Direction

- [ ] The design follows Dense Research Workspace.
- [ ] The design follows `DESIGN.md` colors, radius, spacing, and component rules.
- [ ] The design avoids marketing-first layout, generic AI glow, and fake source proof.

## Responsive And Accessibility

- [ ] Mobile order is search, answer status, answer body, sources, source detail, save/share.
- [ ] No horizontal scrolling or overlapping text appears on mobile.
- [ ] Keyboard order follows the core workflow.
- [ ] Focus states and 44px touch targets are present.
