## Strategy Options

### 1. Enterprise Trust

- Best use case: credible landing page for teams evaluating work knowledge and security.
- Visual tone: restrained, product-led, sharp typography, neutral surfaces, restrained blue accents.
- Structure focus: value first, sourced-answer product UI second, then workflow, integrations, and security.
- Risk: may feel too reserved if the product needs a louder launch tone.
- Best target tools: huashu-design, Claude Design, v0.
- Status: selected.

### 2. Search Copilot Demo

- Best use case: product page that quickly demonstrates the search interaction.
- Visual tone: interactive, product-forward, slightly more energetic.
- Structure focus: example question and sourced answer first, then trust explanation.
- Risk: may over-focus on demo mechanics and under-explain business trust.
- Best target tools: huashu-design, v0.

### 3. Founder-Led Launch

- Best use case: early product launch that needs a sharper narrative around the problem.
- Visual tone: editorial, direct, sparse, with strong copy and fewer interface panels.
- Structure focus: scattered-knowledge pain first, then the product as a focused answer.
- Risk: may feel less mature for enterprise buyers if proof and security are too light.
- Best target tools: Claude Design, huashu-design.

## Final Choice

Use Enterprise Trust for the golden demo because the original request says "官网" and the safest first impression is a credible product-led SaaS page with visible trust cues.
